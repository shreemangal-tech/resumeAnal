## Resume Rubric

### 1. Visual Appeal

#### Highly Satisfactory — 3 marks

ALL of the following are true, with zero exceptions:
• Fits exactly one page.
• No overcrowding (visible white space between sections).
• Any photo used is professional, square-cropped, and passport-style.(not mandatory)
• Font/spacing is uniform across every section.
• No dark shaded blocks or uneven margins.
• The most job-relevant tagline with name/job-role/ Qualification e.g. (Sophomore Developer/Aspiring Tech Enthusiast)

#### Satisfactory — 2 marks

Any one or two of the points mentioned in the “Highly Satisfactory” category were not followed.

#### Average — 1 mark

Any three or four of the points mentioned in the “Highly Satisfactory” category were not followed.

#### Dissatisfactory — 0 marks

None of the points mentioned in the “Highly Satisfactory” category were followed.

### 2. Formatting (ATS)

#### Highly Satisfactory — 3 marks

ALL of the following:
• No tables, text boxes, columns, or embedded images used for layout.
• Exactly one font family throughout.
• Margins between 0.5"–1" on all sides.
• Font size 10–12pt for body text and consistent within each section.
• Special characters/bold/caps used consistently for the same purpose throughout (e.g., all section headers bold, never mixed).
• Bullets used for every list, no paragraph-style lists.
• No borders, shading, or non-standard colors.
